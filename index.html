The combinations

Every result a simple planetary can give. Load a row to see it on the gearset; your chosen input direction carries over.

InputHeldOutputResultDirectionRatio
  • Carrier is the outputReduction. Output turns slower than input, same direction.
  • Carrier is the inputOverdrive. Output turns faster than input, same direction.
  • Carrier is heldReverse. Planets become idlers and flip the direction.
  • Two members lockedDirect drive, 1:1. The set turns as one block.
  • Nothing heldNeutral. No reaction member, so no torque reaches the output.

The math behind every row: S × Nsun + R × Nring = (S + R) × Ncarrier, where S and R are tooth counts and N is speed (clockwise positive).
